1. Arguments are passed in registers a0-a7. 13 is held in a2 (li a2,13 at 0x24 in main).
2. There is no call to f or g in main. The compiler inlined both and folded f(8)+1 into the constant 12 (li a1,12 at 0x26).
3. printf is located at address 0x6f6.
4. ra holds 0x34 just after the jal to printf at 0x30 (address of the next instruction).
5. The output is "He110 World".
6. For big-endian, i would be 0x726c6400. 57616 would not need to change, since %x prints the integer value regardless of byte order.
7. After "y=" an unpredictable value is printed: printf reads the second argument from register a2, which the caller never set, so it prints whatever was in a2.
